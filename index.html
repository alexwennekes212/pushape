PUSHAPE

Push. Ape. Shape. One hundred push-ups, one unbroken set.

Pushape is an iPhone app that trains you to one hundred push-ups in a single set. Kito, an ape who does every rep with you, grows from macaque to silverback as your best set climbs. The Face ID camera counts your reps; an adaptive program writes every session from what you can actually do.
