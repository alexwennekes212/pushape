Pushape Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

The short version

Pushape collects nothing. Your training data never leaves your phone.

What the app stores

Your training history, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device (and in your device backups, which you control). There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections.

The camera

With your permission, Pushape uses the front (Face ID) camera to measure the distance to your face while you do push-ups, which is how reps are counted. This measurement is processed on the device in real time. No photo or video is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Apple Health

If you enable it, Pushape saves finished sessions to Apple Health as workouts. This is off by default, uses Apple's HealthKit on your device, and can be revoked at any time in Settings. Pushape reads nothing from Health.

Notifications

If you enable training reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device.
